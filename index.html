02 — Beyond Her Beauty

Beauty is only the first thing you notice.

Composure

Nothing about her looks rushed. Even in motion, she seems to have already decided how the moment ends.

Presence

Violet eyes, a steady gaze, a posture that asks for nothing. The room adjusts to her, not the reverse.

Ambition

She does not wait to be handed a place. Everything in her bearing says she means to hold it.

Mystery

Her smile is never an answer. It is a question she lets you carry home.

03 — A Mind Like No Other

“She does not play the piece in her hand. She plays the board.”

05 — A Letter to Medea

To Medea Solon,

I have admired many characters for being brave or kind. I admire you for being certain. You walk into every scene as if the ending were already written, and somehow you are the one holding the pen.

Thank you for the elegance, the cleverness, and the little smile that says there is more you have not told us. Never lower your crown for anyone who asks you to.

— Your admirer
